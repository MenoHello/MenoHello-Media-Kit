MenoHello is an independent health education platform focused on perimenopause, menopause, and midlife wellness. Founded by Theresa Mount, a board-certified Pathologists’ Assistant with a background in biology and pathology, the platform makes complex health information easier to understand and apply.

Through in-depth symptom guides, hormone health education, interactive tools, and practical resource navigation, MenoHello helps readers investigate unfamiliar symptoms, understand their options, and make more informed decisions about midlife health.
